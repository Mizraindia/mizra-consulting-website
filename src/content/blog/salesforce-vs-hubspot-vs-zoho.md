# Salesforce vs HubSpot vs Zoho: how to choose the right CRM

**The short answer:**

- Choose **Salesforce** if your sales process is complex, you need deep customisation, or the CRM has to connect to many other systems.
- Choose **HubSpot** if you want the quickest start and your marketing and sales teams will work in one place.
- Choose **Zoho** if you want a lower-cost suite that covers CRM, finance, support and more, and you are happy to set it up around the way you work.

That is a rule of thumb, not a verdict. Features and prices change, so check each vendor's current plans before you decide. The rest of this guide helps you decide in about ten minutes.

## The three tools in a paragraph each

**Salesforce** can be shaped to fit almost any sales process, and it connects to a very wide range of other software. The price of that flexibility is that it needs proper setup, and someone who owns it afterwards.

**HubSpot** is known for being easy to start with. Marketing, sales and customer service tools sit together, so the whole team sees the same customer. It works well when your process is fairly standard. As you add contacts and features the cost can rise, and very unusual processes can be harder to model.

**Zoho** is a broad suite of business apps. Zoho CRM sits next to tools for invoicing, support, projects and custom apps, usually at a lower price. It is flexible and can be built around your process. It has a smaller ecosystem of third-party add-ons than the other two.

## Five questions that decide it

### 1. How unusual is your sales process?

If you sell in a standard way (leads, calls, quotes, deals), any of the three can work. If you have several products, partner channels, approval steps or custom pricing rules, lean towards Salesforce, or a carefully built Zoho.

### 2. Who will look after it?

Every CRM needs an owner. Salesforce rewards a trained admin. HubSpot is friendlier to non-technical owners. Zoho sits in between. If nobody will own it, pick the simplest tool you can live with.

### 3. What does it have to connect to?

List the systems: your website, accounting, support, email and anything else. If you need many integrations with larger systems, Salesforce has the biggest ecosystem. If your finance and support tools can live in the same suite, Zoho can save you a lot of glue work.

### 4. What is the real budget?

Count the licence, the setup, the training and the time of whoever manages it. A cheap licence with no setup can cost more than a pricier tool that fits. Ask each vendor, or a partner, for a quote for your team size and the features you actually need.

### 5. How fast do you need to be live?

HubSpot is usually the quickest to get running. Zoho can be fast when your process is clear. Salesforce takes longer to set up properly. For a typical Salesforce implementation we plan for 8 to 12 weeks.

## The mistake that costs the most

The most expensive mistake is picking the tool before agreeing your process.

Before you compare features, write down in plain words:

- What counts as a customer in your business?
- What stages does a deal go through?
- Who owns each stage?

Teams that skip this tend to move their mess from one system to another. No CRM fixes a process that nobody has agreed. Do this first and the choice often becomes obvious.

## A ten-minute decision checklist

1. Write your sales stages on one page.
2. List the five systems the CRM must connect to.
3. Name the person who will own it.
4. Set a total budget that includes setup and the first year of admin time.
5. Pick the date you need to be live.
6. Ask two vendors or partners for a quote against that one page.

## If you are moving from one CRM to another

Plan the data before you pick the date. Remove duplicates, agree what each field means, run a trial migration in a test environment, and check the result with the people who use the data. Only then migrate for real.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho cheaper than Salesforce?

Often, once you count everything, but the licence is only part of the cost. Compare the same team size and features, and include setup and admin time.

### Which CRM is best for a small business?

The one your team will actually use. For many small teams that means HubSpot or Zoho, for speed and cost. Salesforce can still be right if your process is complex or you plan to grow into it.

### Can I switch later?

Yes, but moving is real work: data, integrations and training. Choosing with your next two or three years in mind can save you a migration.

### Do I need a consultant?

Not always. If your process is standard and your team is small, you may be able to set up HubSpot or Zoho yourselves. A consultant earns the fee when the process is complex, the data is messy, or the CRM has to connect to other systems.
